# ISO 27001:2022 Control Assessment

## Control Information

Control ID: A.5.17
Control Name: Authentication information
Organization: Northstar Digital Solutions
Evidence Type: SIMULATED

## Assessment Objective

Evaluate the allocation and management of
authentication information throughout its
lifecycle.

## Simulated Case Assumptions

- Unique employee credentials are assigned.
- Initial credential distribution is informal.
- Credential storage requirements are incomplete.
- No formal compromised credential revocation
  procedure has been documented.
- Authentication awareness guidance is limited.

These are fictional case assumptions.

## Identified Gaps

GAP-013:
No documented secure credential distribution
procedure.

GAP-014:
Incomplete credential storage and handling
requirements.

GAP-015:
No formal compromised credential revocation
and replacement procedure.

## Assessment Result

Status: PARTIALLY_MET

Rationale:
Basic authentication information management
exists, but secure allocation, handling,
and revocation processes are incomplete.

## Recommended Remediation

1. Establish secure credential distribution.
2. Document credential handling requirements.
3. Adopt approved secrets management practices.
4. Define compromised credential revocation.
5. Provide authentication security awareness.

## Evidence Required for Closure

- Approved credential management procedure
- Secure credential distribution records
- Secrets management configuration evidence
- Credential revocation procedure
- Security awareness completion records
