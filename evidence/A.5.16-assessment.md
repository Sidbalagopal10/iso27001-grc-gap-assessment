# ISO 27001:2022 Control Assessment

## Control Information

Control ID: A.5.16
Control Name: Identity management
Organization: Northstar Digital Solutions
Evidence Type: SIMULATED

## Assessment Objective

Evaluate how user identities are created,
managed, reviewed, and deactivated throughout
their lifecycle.

## Simulated Case Assumptions

- Unique employee identities are assigned.
- Basic user provisioning procedures exist.
- Employee offboarding is inconsistent.
- Dormant accounts are not regularly reviewed.
- Identity lifecycle procedures are incomplete.

These are fictional case assumptions.

## Identified Gaps

GAP-010:
Inconsistent account deactivation during
employee offboarding.

GAP-011:
No documented periodic dormant account reviews.

GAP-012:
Incomplete identity lifecycle procedures.

## Assessment Result

Status: PARTIALLY_MET

Rationale:
Basic identity provisioning exists in the
fictional organization, but identity lifecycle
management is not consistently enforced.

## Recommended Remediation

1. Establish formal identity lifecycle procedures.
2. Integrate HR termination notifications with IT.
3. Define account deactivation timelines.
4. Implement periodic dormant account reviews.
5. Retain provisioning and deactivation evidence.

## Evidence Required for Closure

- Approved identity management procedure
- Employee onboarding and offboarding records
- Account deactivation timestamps
- Dormant account review reports
- Identity lifecycle audit logs
