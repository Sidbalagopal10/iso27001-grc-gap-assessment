# ISO 27001:2022 Control Assessment

## Control Information

Control ID: A.5.1
Control Name: Policies for information security
Organization: Northstar Digital Solutions
Evidence Type: SIMULATED

## Assessment Objective

Evaluate whether information security policies
are established, approved, communicated,
and periodically reviewed.

## Simulated Case Assumptions

The organization has informal security practices.

No formally approved information security policy
has been established in the fictional scenario.

No evidence of policy communication, assigned
policy ownership, or scheduled reviews exists.

These are fictional case assumptions, not
verified findings about a real organization.

## Identified Gaps

GAP-004:
No formally approved information security policy.

GAP-005:
No documented policy communication process.

GAP-006:
No formal policy review and maintenance process.

## Assessment Result

Status: NOT_MET

Rationale:
The fictional organization lacks a formally
established information security policy and
associated governance processes.

## Recommended Remediation

1. Develop an information security policy.
2. Assign policy ownership and responsibilities.
3. Obtain management approval.
4. Communicate the policy to relevant personnel.
5. Establish periodic and change-triggered reviews.

## Evidence Required for Closure

- Approved information security policy
- Management approval record
- Policy communication records
- Assigned policy owner
- Documented review schedule
