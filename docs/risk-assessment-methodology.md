# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Organization

Northstar Digital Solutions

## Assessment Context

This project is an educational, simulated
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A gap assessment.

It evaluates 10 selected Annex A controls
against fictional organizational assumptions.

It is not a complete ISO 27001 audit,
certification assessment, or compliance attestation.

## Assessment Approach

The assessment follows these stages:

1. Define the fictional organization and scope.
2. Identify information assets.
3. Assess selected security controls.
4. Document control weaknesses.
5. Develop plausible risk scenarios.
6. Estimate likelihood and business impact.
7. Calculate inherent risk scores.
8. Identify risk treatment actions.
9. Assign owners and target completion periods.
10. Define evidence required for closure.

## Risk Identification

Each risk record contains:

- Risk identifier
- Associated Annex A control
- Risk scenario
- Threat
- Control weakness
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Risk score
- Risk severity
- Risk owner
- Proposed treatment
- Assessment status
- Evidence classification

Risk scenarios are derived from fictional
case assumptions and identified control gaps.

## Likelihood Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rare: exceptional circumstances required |
| 2 | Unlikely: limited plausible exposure |
| 3 | Possible: credible exposure under normal conditions |
| 4 | Likely: significant exposure or repeated opportunities |
| 5 | Almost Certain: persistent exposure with strong indicators |

Likelihood ratings are qualitative judgments,
not measured incident probabilities.

## Impact Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant: minimal operational consequences |
| 2 | Minor: limited disruption or recoverable loss |
| 3 | Moderate: meaningful service or data impact |
| 4 | Major: significant operational or confidentiality impact |
| 5 | Severe: extensive disruption, sensitive data exposure, or substantial business consequences |

Impact considers confidentiality,
integrity, availability, business operations,
and potential legal or contractual consequences.

## Risk Scoring

Inherent Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

The score ranges from 1 to 25.

## Risk Severity Thresholds

| Score | Severity |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | Low |
| 6-11 | Medium |
| 12-19 | High |
| 20-25 | Critical |

The thresholds are project-defined
and are not prescribed by ISO 27001.

## Inherent Risk

The risk register represents illustrative
inherent-risk estimates for the defined
fictional scenarios.

The assessment does not calculate
residual risk after remediation.

## Risk Treatment

Treatment recommendations prioritize
reducing the likelihood or impact
of the identified scenarios.

Each documented risk is linked to
a planned remediation action.

This one-to-one mapping is a project
design choice, not an ISO requirement.

## Remediation Prioritization

Critical risks receive the highest
initial attention, followed by
High, Medium, and Low risks.

Proposed target days are illustrative.

In a real organization, treatment priorities
would also consider cost, feasibility,
dependencies, business requirements,
and management-approved risk appetite.

## Ownership

Risk owners are responsible for
risk oversight and acceptance decisions.

Remediation owners are responsible
for implementing assigned actions.

The fictional dataset currently assigns
the same individual role to both.

## Evidence Classification

All assessment evidence is labeled
SIMULATED.

Assessment documents describe fictional
control conditions and evidence requirements.

The presence of an assessment document
does not establish that a control was
independently tested or verified.

## Limitations

- Fictional organization and assets
- No production system access
- No independent evidence verification
- No stakeholder interviews
- No operational effectiveness testing
- No formal management risk acceptance
- No residual-risk calculation
- Only 10 selected Annex A controls assessed
- No complete ISO 27001 clauses 4-10 assessment
- No certification or compliance conclusion

## Intended Use

This methodology supports a cybersecurity
portfolio demonstration of risk identification,
control assessment, risk prioritization,
remediation planning, and GRC reporting.
