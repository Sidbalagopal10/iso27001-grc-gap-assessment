# Remediation Planning Assumptions

## Assessment Context

This document supports the simulated ISO/IEC 27001:2022
Annex A gap assessment for Northstar Digital Solutions.

All remediation activities are proposed and have not
been implemented or independently verified.

## Target Days

Target_Days represents the proposed number of calendar
days from formal approval of the remediation plan.

The targets are illustrative and require management
review for feasibility, resources, and dependencies.

## Priority and Scheduling

Critical risks receive the earliest proposed deadlines.

However, severity alone does not establish implementation
feasibility or determine the final remediation sequence.

## Relationship to the 30/60/90-Day Roadmap

Days 1-30:
- Approve the treatment plan and allocate resources.
- Begin and, where feasible, complete 15-day actions.
- Start longer-running remediation activities.
- Record implementation evidence.

Days 31-60:
- Continue outstanding work and validate early actions.
- Complete actions with approved targets in this period.
- Conduct access reviews, recovery tests, and exercises.
- Escalate overdue actions.

Days 61-90:
- Verify sustained control operation.
- Assess residual risks after implementation.
- Address exceptions and obtain risk decisions.
- Update management reporting.

The roadmap is a management planning framework,
not an extension of individual action deadlines.

## Closure Requirements

A remediation action may be marked completed only when
its defined completion criteria are satisfied and
supporting evidence has been reviewed.

## Risk Acceptance

Risk acceptance decisions require authorized management
approval and are not represented as completed in this project.
